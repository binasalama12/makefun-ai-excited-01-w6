# THE FLYING HUG

For months her father was just a voice on a crackly phone call and a date circled in crayon on the kitchen calendar, and then suddenly he was standing in the arrivals hall with his bag still on his shoulder. She did not walk to him — she flew, launching herself into the air with her legs kicked back behind her, and the hand-painted "welcome home" sign she had carried all morning slipped from her hands and clattered to the floor, completely forgotten. They are both laughing and crying at the same time in mid-catch, while the strangers around them slow down, smile, and start to clap for a reunion they only caught the last second of.

The photo is softly graded and stored as entry.png, and main.ts copies it to the output path while recording the sha256 of both files deterministically so the delivered image can always be verified byte for byte.

## Why it excites

- **Jolt:** A small girl is genuinely airborne — body horizontal, legs swung back, arms already open — in the half-second before her father catches her, and that suspended leap hits before you even register where they are.
- **Surprise:** The hand-painted welcome home sign lying dropped on the arrivals floor tells the whole backstory in one detail: she planned this moment carefully, then abandoned the plan the instant she saw him.
- **Momentum:** Everything in the frame leans toward the catch — his knees bending to receive her, her hair and dress still travelling forward, travellers mid-applause — so the still image feels like it is still moving.
- **Thumbnail punch:** Two faces laughing through tears at the centre, one bright dropped sign below them, and a crowd softening into smiles around the edges reads instantly even at the smallest size: she flew home to him.
